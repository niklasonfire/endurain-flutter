# Public GPX fixtures

These three files are unmodified recordings from **Geoprivacy Open Data
2023/11**, with neutral local filenames. The source automatically classifies
these tracks as running. It has already anonymized their dates and endpoints;
relative time intervals remain available. No elevation or heart-rate samples
are included.

Attribution: Mäkinen, V., Brauer, A. and Oksanen, J. (2023), *GeoPrivacy
platform*, Finnish Geospatial Research Institute, National Land Survey of
Finland. Dataset: [Geoprivacy Open Data 2023/11](https://doi.org/10.23729/a86e508a-8798-4720-9b06-0b9db8fc6b8e).
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), with the publisher's
[additional privacy restrictions](https://geoprivacy.fi/open-data-terms-of-use/).
Do not attempt reidentification or link these records to individuals.

See [provenance, original filenames, checksums, and the Geoportti
acknowledgement](../../../docs/screenshot-track-sources.md). The GPX bytes are
unchanged; screenshot metrics are derived from coordinates and timestamps.
